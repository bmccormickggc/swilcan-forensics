Primary Services

Forensic Nursing Expert Witness Services

Swilcan Forensics is available to prosecutors and defense attorneys for initial case consultation, medical record review, written expert opinions, deposition, and trial testimony. Ohio-based and available to travel.

Opinions are based on the available medical records, relevant science, and applicable standards of care. They do not change based on which party retains Swilcan Forensics.

01

Expert Witness Consultation & Testimony

Consultation with retaining counsel regarding forensic nursing matters and clear, well-prepared testimony at deposition, hearing, or trial. Complex medical evidence and forensic nursing practice are explained in accessible language and supported by the available record and relevant science. Travel is available as needed.

02

Medical Record Review & Case Consultation

Review and analysis of medical records, forensic examination documentation, clinical findings, and related case materials. The purpose is to identify what the evidence does and does not support and help counsel understand the clinical issues relevant to the case.

03

Written Expert Reports

Clear, well-supported reports that identify the materials reviewed, explain the relevant medical and forensic nursing concepts, cite supporting literature when appropriate, and state the resulting opinions and their basis.

04

Education, Speaking & Professional Consulting

Customized education, curriculum development, lectures, public speaking, and consultation for universities, nursing schools, medical schools, law schools, healthcare organizations, law enforcement, businesses, and community partners. Topics may include sexual assault, domestic violence, child maltreatment, non-fatal strangulation, medical forensic care, medical documentation, and the effective use of clinical experts.

05

Consulting Projects & Professional Authorship

Project-based consultation for professional publications, protocols, manuals, focus groups, and multidisciplinary or national initiatives related to forensic nursing and the medical response to violence. Tailored consultation is also available to businesses interested in developing protocols for responding to violence in the workplace.

Subject-Matter Expertise

Clinical and Forensic Nursing Expertise

The practice is grounded in medical evidence and forensic nursing standards across pediatric, adolescent, and adult patient populations.

Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner

Dual board certification as a pediatric/adolescent and adult/adolescent Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner, with expertise in medical forensic examinations, clinical documentation, evidence collection, and examination practice.

Domestic Violence

Clinical interpretation of records and findings in domestic violence matters, including injury identification, examination findings, and medical forensic documentation.

Non-Fatal Strangulation

Advanced education in the medical forensic assessment of non-fatal strangulation, including clinical presentation, symptom and injury documentation, and the significance and limitations of both positive and absent findings.

Child Maltreatment

Medical record review and consultation involving pediatric physical and sexual abuse concerns, including evaluation of clinical documentation, findings, and the medical context of the record.

Medical Record Interpretation

Translation of medical terminology, examination findings, and clinical documentation into clear explanations for counsel, judges, and juries, including what the record can and cannot establish.

Professional Standards & Education

Consultation and education concerning forensic nursing practice, documentation, evidence collection, program development, curriculum design, and evidence-based professional standards.

About Selena McCormick

Selena McCormick, MSN, RN, CPN, SANE-P, SANE-A is the founder and principal of Swilcan Forensics. She is a master's-prepared registered nurse and holds national board certifications as a Certified Pediatric Nurse, Pediatric/Adolescent Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner, and Adult/Adolescent Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner.

She earned a Bachelor of Science in Public Health from Saint Louis University, a Bachelor of Science in Nursing from New York University, and a Master of Science in Nursing with a focus in Nursing Executive Leadership from Northern Kentucky University. She is bilingual in English and Spanish and fluent in French.

Her clinical and educational background includes the medical forensic care of patients who have experienced sexual assault, domestic violence, human trafficking, child maltreatment, and non-fatal strangulation. Her work encompasses medical forensic examinations, evidence collection and documentation, multidisciplinary collaboration, professional education, and the interpretation of complex medical records.

Selena's role as an expert is deliberately focused and objective: to explain the medical evidence, the relevant science, and the limits of the record. She does not determine whether an alleged event occurred, and her opinions do not depend on which party retained her.

She has presented research and lectured nationally and has authored professional publications addressing forensic nursing and the medical response to violence.

Swilcan Forensics serves prosecutors and defense attorneys and also provides education, speaking, curriculum development, professional authorship, and project-based consulting to legal, healthcare, academic, business, law enforcement, and community organizations.
